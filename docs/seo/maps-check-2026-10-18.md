Sunshine has the best rating and the most reviews in the list, yet ranks below shops with 1 to 6
reviews, even with the map centred on its own door. Likely reasons, in order:

1. **Missing category for drop-off service.** Smile Wash, which leads "ซักอบพับ" and "ฝากซักผ้า",
   is categorised "บริการซักรีด" (Laundry service). Sunshine has only "Laundromat", so Maps does
   not consider it for wash-and-fold searches at all.
2. **Name has no search words.** Competitors' Maps names contain "ร้านสะดวกซัก", "24 ชม.".
   Sunshine's is "Sunshine ซัก+อบ". Do not stuff words into the name (Google suspends listings for
   that); only change it if the shop sign itself says more, e.g. "Sunshine Wash & Dry".
3. **Name differs from the website** ("Sunshine Wash & Dry"), which weakens Google's link between
   the two.
4. Services, description and weekend hours are not filled in (to confirm in the profile).
