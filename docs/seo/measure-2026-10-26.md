# SEO / AEO measurement, 2026-10-26

Nine days after the site changes (2026-10-17) and the same day the Google Business Profile
(the shop's Google Maps listing) was edited. Method: `seo-aeo-framework` skill, steps 1 and 8.
Numbers over time live in `tracking/`; this file explains them.

## Summary

- **Google Maps is the big mover.** With the map centred on the shop, Sunshine is now first for
  ร้านสะดวกซัก, ซักอบพับ and ซักผ้านวม, and second for ร้านซักผ้า and ฝากซักผ้า. On 2026-10-18
  it was 5th to 7th and missing for ซักอบพับ. Checked twice a few minutes apart; the two biggest
  moves held.
- **Google AI answers (AI Mode) name Sunshine in 2 of 10 questions, first both times.** It misses
  every wash-and-fold question; ironing shops and pickup services win those.
- **Search Console, first 9 days:** 237 appearances in Google, 1 click, average position 7.4. All
  6 pages are indexed. The home page also appeared 12 times inside Google's AI answers.
- **Normal Google results:** the new pages now rank. The comforter page is #1 for ซักผ้านวม บางพลี
  and the weekend page #2 for ซักอบพับ เสาร์อาทิตย์ (both not on page 1 before). All 15 terms
  measured; 9 of them later the same day, after Google lifted a temporary block.
