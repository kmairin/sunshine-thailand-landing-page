Next · Reviews

A review card for the counter

  1. Print it and put it at the counter and on the folding table.
  2. Customers scan it; it opens your Google review page.
  3. Ask them to say which service they used: ฝากซักอบพับ, ผ้านวม.

Ask every customer, not only happy ones, and never give a reward for a review. Google removes those reviews and can penalise the listing.

Aim: named in wash-and-fold AI answers by the 17 November check.
