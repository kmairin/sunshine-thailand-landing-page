FAQ

Comforter washing questions

How much does it cost to wash a comforter?
The 18 kg washer is 60 THB cold, 70 THB warm, 80 THB hot. The dryer is 50 THB per 28 minutes; extra time is 10 THB per 6 minutes.
Can I use the 14 kg washer for a comforter?
We recommend the 18 kg washer for comforters and blankets so they tumble freely and come out clean. Not sure? Send us a photo on LINE.
How long should I dry a comforter?
Start with 28 minutes, then add 1–2 top-ups (6 minutes, 10 THB each) until it is completely dry.
Can I come at night?
Yes. We are open 24 hours a day, every day, fully lit with CCTV.
Can I drop off a comforter instead?
Our weekend wash & fold drop-off runs Saturday and Sunday, 9am–8pm, with no service fee. For comforters, message us on LINE first.
