FAQ

Things customers ask us

Do you offer drop-off wash, dry & fold service?
Yes — every Saturday and Sunday from 9am to 8pm we run a free drop-off Wash, Dry & Fold service. No service fee. You only pay for the wash and dry. Just add us on LINE, send a photo of your laundry basket, choose your wash and dry program, and our staff will wash, dry, and fold for you. Details in the drop-off section.
How much does drop-off wash & fold cost?
The drop-off service itself is free — no service charge. Customers only pay the wash and dry rates: starting at 50 THB for a 14kg cold wash and 50 THB for a 28-minute dry.
Can I drop off laundry on weekdays?
Drop-off Wash, Dry & Fold is currently available weekends only — Saturday and Sunday, 9am–8pm. On weekdays the shop runs as a 24-hour self-service laundromat (coin and PromptPay QR).
What are your hours?
The laundromat is open 24 hours a day, every day, for self-service. Our staffed drop-off Wash, Dry & Fold service runs Sat & Sun only, 9am–8pm.
How much does a wash cost?
Washers start at 50 THB for a 14kg cold cycle. Dryers are 50 THB for 28 minutes. Drop-off wash, dry & fold (weekends) is free of service charge. Full table in the Pricing section.
Is there staff or is it self-service?
Weekdays: fully self-service, 24 hours a day. Saturdays and Sundays, 9am–8pm: staff are on-site for the free drop-off wash, dry & fold service. Outside those hours, walk in and use the machines yourself any time. Need help? Message us on LINE.
What payment methods do you accept? PromptPay?
Both coins (5 and 10 THB) and PromptPay QR via any Thai banking app. No more carrying heavy coins.
Can I wash a comforter or large blanket?
Yes — use the 18 kg machine so the comforter can tumble freely.
How long does a wash & dry take?
Wash 30–45 min, dry 28 min per cycle (add 6 min for 10 THB). Plan for about 1 hr 15 min total.
How close is Suvarnabhumi Airport and Mega Bangna?
5 km from Mega Bangna (10 min drive), 10 km from Suvarnabhumi Airport (15 min). Convenient for travelers, airline crew, and Bangna-Trad commuters.
