FAQ

Things customers ask us

What are your hours?
Open 24 hours a day, every day of the year. Walk in any time.
How much does a wash cost?
Washers start at 50 THB for a 14kg cold cycle. Dryers are 50 THB for 28 minutes. Full table in the Pricing section.
Is there staff or is it self-service?
Fully self-service. Walk in, follow the instructions on each machine, and message us on LINE if you need help.
What payment methods do you accept? PromptPay?
Both coins (5 and 10 THB) and PromptPay QR via any Thai banking app. No more carrying heavy coins.
Can I wash a comforter or large blanket?
Yes — use the 18 kg machine so the comforter can tumble freely.
How long does a wash & dry take?
Wash 30–45 min, dry 28 min per cycle (add 6 min for 10 THB). Plan for about 1 hr 15 min total.
How close is Suvarnabhumi Airport and Mega Bangna?
5 km from Mega Bangna (10 min drive), 10 km from Suvarnabhumi Airport (15 min). Convenient for travelers, airline crew, and Bangna-Trad commuters.
