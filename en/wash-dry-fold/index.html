FAQ

Wash & fold questions

How much is wash and fold?
There is no service fee. You pay only the machines: for example a 14 kg cold wash (50 THB) plus one 28-minute dry (50 THB) is 100 THB per load.
When can I drop off laundry?
Drop-off runs every Saturday and Sunday, 9am–8pm. Self-service machines are open 24 hours, every day.
Can I drop off on a weekday?
Not yet: drop-off is weekends only. On weekdays you can use the machines yourself 24/7 and pay by coin or PromptPay QR.
How do I order?
Add us on LINE, send a photo of your basket and choose the wash (cold, warm or hot) and dry programs. Staff wash, dry and fold it and message you on LINE when it is ready.
Can I pick up after 8pm?
Yes. Folded laundry waits on the in-store shelf and you can collect it any time, 24/7.
How do I pay?
At the shop, in cash or by PromptPay QR from any Thai banking app.
Can you wash comforters?
Comforters go in the 18 kg washer (60–80 THB). See comforter washing. To drop off a comforter, message us on LINE first.
